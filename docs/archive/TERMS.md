# Vocabulary

The project's terms, each defined once and used consistently. CI lints prose against this list.
Notation follows `model/SPEC.md` M0 (proposal section 3); a later model version may specialize a term but not redefine it.

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Review date | A quarterly decision date t; the only times the investor trades. | proposal §2 |
| Active fund | A mutual fund with factor loadings B^A and residual expected return alpha, net of internal costs. | M0 |
| ETF | A passive fund with loadings B^E and fee-and-tracking drag c^E; zero ETF alpha is a modelling assumption. | M0 |
| Economic factor | One of K attribution factors with return f and conditional mean lambda (the factor premium); need not be tradable. | M0 |
| Factor premium | lambda_t, the conditional mean of factor returns given the latent state. | M0 |
| Alpha | The active-fund residual expected return relative to the economic factor model, net of internal costs; never defined relative to the ETF menu. | proposal §3.1 |
| Exposure | b(a, p) = (B^A)' a + (B^E)' p for positions normalized by pre-trade wealth. | M0 |
| Action class | One of: no trade; ETF-only (active units or pre-trade-normalized active dollars fixed); full trading. | proposal §3.2 |
| ETF-only feasible exposure set | The image under b of all affordable, mandate-compliant ETF adjustments with active holdings fixed. | proposal §3.2 |
| Missing direction | An exposure direction outside the linear span of the ETF loadings. | proposal §3.2 |
| Infeasible matching | An exposure change in the ETF span that requires prohibited borrowing or shorting. | proposal §3.2 |
| Switching cost | A shareholder-level cost of trading a fund (load, redemption fee, commission, spread); distinct from internal fund trading costs, which NAV returns already include. | proposal §2 |
| One-quarter score | Q_t(w; theta), the conditional mean-variance surrogate net of investor trading costs; not a multiperiod solution. | proposal §3.3 |
| Return risk | Variability of realized returns given the parameters (Sigma). | proposal §3.3 |
| Estimation uncertainty | Uncertainty about conditional means (lambda, alpha) given the data (Omega); not return covariance. | proposal §3.3 |
| Ambiguity aversion | A preference for protection against model or parameter misspecification; distinct from the two above. | proposal §3.3 |
| Certificate | A lower bound on L_t(w) valid on a stated coverage event; an optimizer's incumbent is not one. | proposal §4B |
| Best-arm identification | The statistical task of identifying the best among several alternatives with controlled error; general lower bounds compare information against alternatives that would change the best choice. The paper's fixed public histories and funded ETF action class are not literally the adaptive arm-sampling settings of the cited results. | `garivier2016optimal`; `kaufmann2014complexity` |
| Option value of future flexibility | The effect of a future trading opportunity on the value of an action chosen today; its sign for an active-trading advantage depends on which root and future action classes are compared. | paper Propositions 12--13 |
| Hedging demand | A current exposure choice that changes because future investment opportunities and information will change; the two-review examples show only specific comparator signs, not a general demand formula. | paper Propositions 12--13 |
| Two-stage procedure | Choose factor exposures first, then managers, as specified in a claim; the comparator is the joint optimum. In M2 (claim 027): stage 1 chooses a factor exposure from lambda_bar, Sigma_f and gamma alone, the unconstrained target b_TB = Sigma_f^{-1} lambda_bar/gamma or the feasibility-aware target b* maximizing G on B_F; stage 2 chooses w in F with b(w) = b* maximizing H. Its value is T = G(b*) + V(b*) and its loss Lambda = J - T against the joint optimum J under identical forecasts. | proposal §4C; M2, claim 027 |
| Conjectured | Evidence status: stated, no proof; numerical support at most. | AGENTS.md rule 5 |
| Paper-only | A claim approved with a reviewed paper proof and deliberately not formalized (PM decision). | AGENTS.md rule 6b |
| Fixed-mean parameter and belief | In M1, theta = (lambda_1, lambda_2, alpha) belongs to a nonempty finite set Theta; pi is the given joint decision-date probability distribution on Theta, not a confidence region. | M1 |
| Return scenario | In M1, s in a finite set S has probability q_s independent of theta and holdings; z^f_s, z^A_s and z^E_s are jointly specified zero-mean shocks. | M1 |
| Conditional return mean and shock | In M1, mu(theta) is the vector of conditional instrument means; xi_s = r_s(theta) - mu(theta), with Sigma = sum_s q_s xi_s xi_s'. | M1 |
| Pre-trade wealth and normalized holdings | W^- = h^- + sum_i x^-_i > 0; w^- = x^- / W^- = (a^-, p^-), w = x^+ / W^- = (a, p), v = u / W^- and k^- = h^- / W^- in M1. | M1 |
| Normalized switching cost and cash | In M1, kappa_i is a proportional shareholder cost rate, tau(v) = sum_i kappa_i abs(v_i), and k(w) = k^- - sum_i v_i - tau(v) is post-trade cash divided by W^-. | M1 |
| Position limit | In M1, bar w_i bounds instrument i's post-trade holdings as a fraction of pre-trade wealth; the initial holdings satisfy this bound. | M1 |
| Named action classes | In M1, F is the full feasible class, E its subset fixing a = a^-, and N = {w^-} denotes no trade. | M1 |
| ETF exposure sets | In M1, B_E is the ETF-only feasible exposure set, D_E its changes from b(w^-), and L_E the linear span of ETF loadings without funding, sign or position constraints. | M1 |
| Exposure matching | An ETF-only action v matches a full action w when b(v) = b(w); this alone does not mean equality of returns, risk, costs or objective. | M1 |
| Terminal marked wealth | W_1(w; theta, s) is cash plus the gross value of held fund units at the end of M1's quarter, without compulsory liquidation. | M1 |
| Belief-average conditional score | Qbar_0(w) = sum_theta pi_theta Q_0(w; theta) in M1; gamma is the nonnegative conditional return-risk coefficient. This is not a score using full predictive variance. | M1 |
| Directional proportional switching costs | In M2, kappa^+_i and kappa^-_i in [0, 1) charge instrument i's purchase and sale, respectively, on the gross change in holdings at the review quote, before fees. C_0(u) = sum_i (kappa^+_i u_i^+ + kappa^-_i u_i^-); tau uses the same formula for normalized trades v. These replace only M1's symmetric cost definitions. | M2 |
| Positive and negative trade parts | In M2, u_i^+ = max(u_i, 0), u_i^- = max(-u_i, 0), and likewise for v. These superscripts denote nonnegative parts of the signed trade, not pre-trade and post-trade holdings. | M2 |
| Paired mean error | For holdings w = (a, p) and comparator v = (a_v, p_v), d_b = b(w) - b(v), d_a = a - a_v, e_lambda = hat lambda - lambda and e_alpha = hat alpha - alpha. The paired mean error is the estimated score difference minus the original score difference. | M0; claim 002 |
| Estimated-mean score | hat Q_t evaluates the M0 score with hat lambda and hat alpha replacing lambda and alpha, while loadings, covariance, drag, risk coefficient, initial holdings and cost function remain fixed. | M0; claim 002 |
| Self-financing identity | At a review, post-trade risky holdings plus cash plus the shareholder switching cost equal initial risky holdings plus cash, under M0's funding equations. | M0; claim 001 |
| Normalized terminal gain | In M2, G_s(w; theta) = W_1(w; theta, s)/W^- - 1, with initial wealth as denominator and no terminal liquidation cost. | M2; claim 003 |
| Finite conditional moments | For scenario masses q in M2, E_q X = sum_s q_s X_s and Var_q X = sum_s q_s (X_s - E_q X)^2, with theta held fixed. | M2; claim 003 |
| Belief mean | bar theta = sum_theta pi_theta theta, with components bar lambda and bar alpha. Evaluation of M2's affine return or score formula at bar theta does not assert that bar theta belongs to Theta. | M2; claim 003 |
| Convex action class | A class containing t w + (1-t) z whenever it contains w and z and 0 <= t <= 1. | M2; claim 004 |
| Concave score | A score H with H(t w + (1-t) z) >= t H(w) + (1-t) H(z) for 0 <= t <= 1 on a convex domain. | M2; claim 004 |
| Attained class maximum | A finite score value realized by a feasible holding and at least as large as the score of every other holding in that action class. V_D denotes the supremum of Qbar_0 on class D; attainment says a holding in D realizes V_D. | M2; claim 004 |
| ETF trade and feasible trade set | In M2, d = p - p^- is the ETF trade while a = a^-; bar p is the ETF part of bar w, and P_E is the set of d satisfying -p^- <= d <= bar p - p^- and Psi_E(d) <= k^-. | M2; claim 005 |
| ETF net cash outlay | Psi_E(d) = sum_j [d_j + kappa^+_{E,j} max(d_j, 0) + kappa^-_{E,j} max(-d_j, 0)], with kappa^+_{E,j} and kappa^-_{E,j} the ETF purchase and sale rates. It can be negative when sales raise cash. | M2; claim 005 |
| ETF cash inequalities | For each assignment sigma of purchase or sale to the ETFs, c_{sigma,j} is 1 + kappa^+_{E,j} or 1 - kappa^-_{E,j}, respectively. The simultaneous inequalities c_sigma' d <= k^- represent M2's ETF-only cash condition. | M2; claim 005 |
| Target exposure change | delta in R^2 is a target for (B^E)' d; when comparing a full action w to the incumbent, delta = b(w) - b(w^-). | M2; claim 005 |
| Complete feasible exposure substitution | Every holding in F has a holding in E with the same b; this alone asserts no equality of risk, cash, cost, terminal wealth or score. | M2; claim 006 |
| Total risky holding in one-ETF replacement examples | t = a + p in claims 006 and 008, normalized by pre-trade wealth. | M2; claims 006, 008 |
| Replacement holding | T(w) = (0,a+p) replaces the active holding w=(a,p) by the same normalized dollar amount of the sole ETF in claim 008; its feasibility and score comparison require the stated assumptions. | M2; claim 008 |
| Residual shock difference | epsilon_s = xi_A,s - xi_E,s for claim 008's equal-loading instruments; v_epsilon = sum_s q_s epsilon_s^2. Its zero cross-moment with xi_E is an additional hypothesis. | M2; claim 008 |
| Exact return replication | Active and ETF returns agree for every allowed parameter and scenario; equal loadings alone do not imply this, nor does return equality imply equal trading costs or feasible replacement. | M2; claim 008 |
| Zero-active non-participation | A post-review holding a=0; distinct from no active trade a=a^- when an incumbent is present, and from uniqueness of the whole portfolio. | M2; claim 008 |
| Common-return score in the zero-alpha examples | H(t)=t/50-t^2/200 before shareholder review costs, for the four assumed identical-return cases in claim 008. | M2; claim 008 |
| Score certificate residual | R_i = g_i - eta_B - (1 + eta_B)t_i, where g is the smooth score marginal and t is a switching-cost subgradient at a candidate holding. Its signs at free position coordinates provide a sufficient global score inequality in claim 007; the proof does not assume a KKT necessity theorem. | M2; claim 007 |
| Budget multiplier | eta_B >= 0 multiplies k(w) in the maximization Lagrangian Qbar_0(w) + eta_B k(w); complementary slackness requires eta_B k(w) = 0. | M2; D2 band candidate in FINDINGS |
| Smooth score marginal | g_i = mu_i(bar theta) - gamma (Sigma w)_i excludes the switching-cost kink; at an interior position with cost subgradient t_i, stationarity is g_i = eta_B + (1 + eta_B)t_i. | M2; D2 band candidate in FINDINGS |
| No-active-trade band | Values of the active mean alpha for which some full-class maximizer has a=a^- with other inputs fixed; without uniqueness this does not constrain every maximizer. Any use must specify the admissible alpha domain. C is the set for the affine score extension in claim 009. | M2; D2 candidate and claim 009 |
| Compatible budget multiplier interval | I consists of the nonnegative budget multipliers consistent with the ETF coordinates' stationarity, position-bound conditions and cash complementarity at a fixed ETF-only optimum w_E; lo is its lower endpoint and hi its upper endpoint, possibly infinity. Claim 009 states its existence and when finiteness holds; eta abbreviates eta_B there. | M2; D2 candidate and claim 009 |
| Translated-alpha family | In claim 009, x is the active belief mean obtained by translating all support alphas by x-bar alpha_0. J=(alpha_min,infinity) is the domain where all active gross returns remain positive; scores outside J are only an algebraic extension. | M2; claim 009 |

| ETF cost-slope endpoints | ell_j and u_j are the lower and upper one-coordinate switching-cost subgradients at p_E,j-p^-_j; the interval collapses away from a trade kink. | M2; claim 009 |
| Extended band endpoints | In claim 009, b=kappa^+_A, s=kappa^-_A, L=alpha_c-s+(1-s)lo and U=alpha_c+b+(1+b)hi when hi is finite; the no-active-trade set also depends on active position bounds and the admissible domain J. | M2; claim 009 |
