# Research archive

The lab paper is a preserved research record, broader than the edited article.
Its historical title/date lines and internal claim labels remain unchanged.
It includes abandoned directions, restricted results and qualifications that
are useful when tracing the development. It is not a second polished article.

The integrated lab analysis has different assumed costs, forecasts and starting
holdings from the article's controlled forecast-revision examples. Its gains
must not be pooled with theirs or described as corroborating the same calibration.
The manuscript discussion of alpha, fee savings, horizons and small incremental
planning gains remains the interpretation of its own cases.

Original study code is preserved under `research/` with native relative paths.
The selected manuscript studies and the integrated analysis include saved
outputs. Other historical source runners and checks are supplied for provenance;
not every historical generated dataset or full experimental run is bundled.
Third-party full-text articles are excluded, as are scheduler configuration,
private inboxes and compiled dependency caches.
